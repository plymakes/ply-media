One spelling per name, everywhere (cards, captions, alt text). Basketball: FIBA's spelling. Football: UEFA (Cyprus) or the AFC/FIFA match sheet (Lebanon). Athletics: World Athletics. Add new names with the source of the spelling and the date. Snapshot 19 Oct 2026; details and caveats in `lebanon.md` / `cyprus.md` section 6.
