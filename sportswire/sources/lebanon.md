# SportsWire LB: Source Map (checked 19 Oct 2026)

> **Research snapshot, 19 Oct 2026.** Use this file to know *where to look*. Results, dates and names in it are leads, not verified facts: every fact on a post still has to pass PLAYBOOK section 4 on the day. Items marked UNCONFIRMED have not been checked at all. The weekly desk keeps this file current (dead sites, new handles, new season dates).
>
> Fetching note: some sites refuse automated fetching from the newsroom's environment (seen 19 Oct: philenews.com / in-cyprus, the961.com, lorientlejour.com/sport, Transfermarkt, Kathimerini Cyprus). Use web search snippets only to find the story, then confirm on a site that opens. Never try to get around a block.


**How this was checked:** I opened every URL listed here myself, or saw it directly in a search result, between 18 and 19 Oct 2026. Anything I could not check is marked **UNCONFIRMED**. Instagram blocks automated fetching, so the handles were checked through HypeAuditor's public profile pages or through links on a club or federation's own website.

## 3. Low-reliability and rumour sources (never use alone)
- **Betting and prediction sites** that dominate search results for Lebanon matches: sportytrader.com, scores24.live, oddslot.com, ratingbet.com, livetipsportal.com, sportsdunia.com, bettingpro.com.au. The only source I found for **Maldives 0-6 Lebanon (1 Oct 2026)** was sportytrader, so that score is **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Fan accounts**, e.g. @waelarakji16 ("Wael Arakji Fan Page", 10.2K).
- **@lebbball** ("Lebanese Basketball", 86K). Ownership is unclear and it is probably a media or fan page. Do not treat it as the federation.
- **AI and spam content farms** appearing in search: vibbra.station.orbital.company ("Lebanon national basketball team overview"), magazin.perspective.co, seabase.core.cli.mbl.edu.
- **TheSportsDB** (user-contributed) and **stale Kooora pages**.
- **Wikipedia**: fine for orientation, but it lags and contradicts itself. Its "Lebanon at the 2026 Asian Games" page still says the Games have not happened, while its medal table lists Lebanon 0-1-1. Never cite it for fresh results.
