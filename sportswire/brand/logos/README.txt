SPORTSWIRE BRAND ASSETS - FINAL
===============================
Identity: rounded rising bars (10deg lean) + Rubik ExtraBold lowercase wordmark.
Light-first: bone #F3F1EB surfaces primary; ink #16171B is the dark alternate.

COLORS
  Ink #16171B | Bone #F3F1EB | White #FAFAF8
  LB signal red #FF3B30 (all backgrounds)
  CY amber #E38C00 on light / #FFB300 on dark

TYPE (Google Fonts)
  Rubik ExtraBold lowercase - wordmark & headlines (incl. Arabic for .lb)
  Manrope - captions/body | JetBrains Mono Bold - scores, times
  Lockup/wordmark files have text converted to outlines - no font needed.

FILES (SVG = source of truth, PNG = ready to upload)
  avatar-{lb,cy}-light ......... IG profile pictures (1000px PNG, bone bg)
  avatar-{lb,cy}-dark-alt ...... dark avatar alternates
  app-tile-{lb,cy}-bone ........ tiles for stickers, highlight covers
  app-tile-{lb,cy}-ink-alt ..... dark tiles for photo/video overlays
  app-tile-*-favicon-{64,180} .. favicon / apple-touch sizes
  lockup-horizontal-* .......... mark + wordmark, light & dark, per country
  lockup-stacked-* ............. centered vertical lockup (square-ish uses)
  wordmark-* ................... type-only, light & dark, per country
  mark-*-on-{light,dark} ....... transparent bars only
  mark-mono-{white,ink}-watermark .. 55%/45% opacity post watermarks

RULES
  - Accent once per layout: tall bar OR suffix OR one element
  - Never rotate, outline, gradient, or re-slant the bars
  - Clearspace = one bar-width around mark and lockups
