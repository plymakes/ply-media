### LB: 2000 AFC Asian Cup in Lebanon
- Lebanon hosted the 2000 Asian Cup (AFC, tier 1): https://www.the-afc.com/en/national/afc_asian_cup/news/troussier_2000_triumph_an_amazing_memory.html
- Played it "as the host country"; first qualification by merit was 2019 (AFC): https://www.the-afc.com/en/national/afc_asian_cup/news/ones_to_watch_hassan_matouk_lebanon.html
- Final Sun 29 Oct 2000, Japan 1-0 Saudi Arabia, Mochizuki from Nakamura's free kick (29' per JFA/RSSSF, 30' per Japan Times: leave the minute out or use JFA): http://www.jfa.jp/samuraiblue/asiancup_history/ · https://rsssf.org/tables/00asch-ext.html · https://www.japantimes.co.jp/sports/2000/10/31/soccer/j-league/japan-edges-saudi-arabia-to-become-asian-champion/ · https://www.insideworldfootball.com/2024/01/03/asian-cup-2000/
- Final at the Sports City Stadium, Beirut (now Camille Chamoun Sports City Stadium). Japan's 2nd title; coach Philippe Troussier; MVP Hiroshi Nanami; top scorer Lee Dong-gook, 6 (JFA).
- Lebanon's group: 0-4 Iran, 2-2 Iraq (from 2-0 down), 1-1 Thailand; 3rd in the group on 2 points, out (RSSSF + Inside World Football).
- UNCONFIRMED: match dates 12/15/18 Oct, Lebanon's scorers, attendance at the final (49,500, Japan Times only), Tripoli as a host city, Lebanon's coach.
- Photo: Commons "Camille Chamoun Sports City Stadium 2015 (crop).jpg", CC BY-SA 4.0, Captainm (checked with tools/archive_photo.py info, OK, 926x617). Avoid the "Beirut derby (Nejmeh fans)" file (one club's fans).

### LB: Lebanon 29-18 France, RLWC 2017

### CY: Baghdatis wins Stockholm, 25 Oct 2009
- Final Sun 25 Oct 2009, beat Olivier Rochus 6-1, 7-5: official Stockholm Open history https://www.stockholmopen.se/en/event-info/2000-2009 · UPI https://www.upi.com/Sports_News/2009/10/25/Baghdatis-wins-Stockholm-Open/42901256509050/ · AFP via The Local https://www.thelocal.se/20091025/22870
- Third ATP title, first since Zagreb in February 2007 (UPI + AFP). Söderling walkover in the semi (Stockholm Open + Globe and Mail). Royal Tennis Hall, his first Stockholm event, age 24 (Stockholm Open). 15 aces (UPI + AFP).
- Career: four ATP titles; 2006 Australian Open final; 2006 Wimbledon semi-final; career-high No. 8; retired after Wimbledon 2019 (Stadium Astro + Greek Herald).

### CY: APOEL 2-1 Porto, 1 Nov 2011
- Tue 1 Nov 2011, Group G, GSP Stadium: APOEL 2-1 Porto; Aílton penalty (first half); Hulk penalty a minute from time; Gustavo Manduca 90' from Constantinos Charalambides' cross; APOEL top on 8 points, four ahead of Porto (UEFA https://uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01f7-0ea3a5918065-7fb578fa1236-1000--apoel-s-manduca-floors-porto-late-on + AP via Hürriyet https://hurriyetdailynews.com/apoels-euro-fairytale-continues-6738)

- UNCONFIRMED: attendance; APOEL finishing top of the group (RSSSF only).
